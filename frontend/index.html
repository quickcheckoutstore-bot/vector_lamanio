Move without dragging · How to use

Drag a small shade into a circle to group it, or into empty space to split it out. Drag the circle's rim onto another circle to join groups. Every drop joins or separates circles without recoloring. A compact bubble stays together when dragged out. Show all shades to drag an exact color. Split separates selected colors; Restore merged shades brings back colors combined by Merge.
